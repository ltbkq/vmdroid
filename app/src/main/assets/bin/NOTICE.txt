Purpose in this app
-------------------
The VMDroid app installs `.img.zst` compressed system images. It performs
streaming decompression by executing this statically linked binary as a
subprocess: `ProcessBuilder(zstd, "-dc")` with the compressed source bytes
fed on stdin (see the header comment of
app/src/main/java/io/github/ltbkq/vmdroid/systemimage/ZstdInflator.kt).

Asset path:  app/src/main/assets/bin/zstd
Runtime:     extracted by SystemImageRepository.ensureZstdBinary() into
             filesDir/bin/zstd and made executable (chmod +x); if the asset
             is missing, resolution falls back to a zstd found on PATH
             (development-only fallback).
